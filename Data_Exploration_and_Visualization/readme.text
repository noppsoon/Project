# Data Exploration and Visualization

Projects exploring data through interactive dashboards and visualisations.

## Projects

### Economic and Environmental Impacts of Deforestation
An interactive dashboard exploring environmental and economic indicators across countries, regions, and income groups from 2000 to 2020. Includes trend charts, geographical comparisons, and scatter and bubble plots.

### Spotlight on Melbourne: Events Through Time
An interactive dashboard exploring performing arts events across Melbourne. Includes a year range filter, a map of event venues, and a comparison of the top 10 venues by event count.

See each project folder for further details and files.
